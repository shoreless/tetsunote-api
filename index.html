Open data. Endless journeys.

Tetsunote is a railway logbook. This atlas explores its reference dataset: operators, registered railway lines, station records, and the track segments that connect them.

Distances are calculated from geometry, not official operating distances. Line colors come from the dataset where available, with consistent fallback colors for other lines. English names are included where available; Japanese names are preserved throughout.

The v0 format is experimental. The source snapshot is N02-25; this is not a live service or timetable. Some junctions remain unresolved.

Railways: processed from National Land Numerical Information, MLIT Japan. See the manifest and attribution. Coastlines: Natural Earth, public domain.
